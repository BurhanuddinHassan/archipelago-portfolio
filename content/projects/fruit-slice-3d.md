## What it is
A browser fruit-slicing game drawn in a black-ink sumi-e style, built with Three.js. Raycasting detects which fruit your swipe cuts.

## How it plays
- **60-second rounds, 3 lives.** Swipe across the melon to start.
- **Scoring:** +10 a fruit, +5 each when you cut 3 or more in one swipe, and a rare golden fruit worth +50 that triggers a ×10 combo multiplier. Hitting a bomb ends the game.
- **Player accounts** with a personal dashboard.
