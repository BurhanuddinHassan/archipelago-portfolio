## The story
Aiden Cross, a soldier from 2145, sees from his ship that the timeline split during the Second World War: enemy soldiers got hold of alien technology. Time only flows one way, so once a split happens the new future takes over. Aiden goes back to fight through the enemy soldiers, take the alien tech back before the new timeline settles, and face the final boss.

## My role
Our first complete game, made by a team of three as the final project for two university courses. I worked mainly on gameplay programming in Unity (C#).

## What I built
- **Enemy AI as a state machine:** soldiers patrol, detect the player, chase, shoot, and return to their patrol route once the player is out of sight.
- **Player shooting** mechanics and core gameplay scripting.
- **Scene and level setup** across the 2145 and WWII settings.
