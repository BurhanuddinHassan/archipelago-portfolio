## The story
The dive starts in an underwater base. The team searches three places on the seabed and finds nothing. On the way to the last spot a giant whale swims across in front of them and they have to wait. They finally find the treasure, and the moment they open it a mosasaurus rises up and swallows them. The end.

## My role
VR programmer on a team of four, for our university VR course final project. I built the swimming system.

## What I built
- **Arm-stroke swimming:** players move through the water the way they would in real life, by making swimming strokes with their hands and controllers.

## Result
A complete VR experience with a full story from the base to the ending.
