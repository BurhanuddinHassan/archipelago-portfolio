## The problem
A university VR project: survive and explore underwater in first person, on a standalone headset with tight performance limits.

## How I built it
- **Neutral-buoyancy locomotion** so moving underwater feels weightless.
- **Socket interactions** for picking up and attaching gear, built on the XR Interaction Toolkit.
- **Boids flocking** for fish schools.
- **Diegetic, in-world UI** to keep the player immersed.
