## The idea
The jam theme was "Mask". In our town, every person wears the same smiling mask and never shows a real emotion, even when they are scared or sad. The player is an angel who removes the masks, sees how each person really feels, and helps them. One villager is terrified of a spider nearby: take his mask off, see the fear, and deal with the spider. The game's message is that it's okay to express your feelings.

## My role
Gameplay programmer on a team of six, over 48 hours. A teammate made the smiling mask model.

## What I built
- **Character movement and interactions** in C#, including the interactions the story depends on.
- **Animation triggers** between idle and walking.
- **Event-driven UI** that shows and hides interface elements when story events fire.

## Result
Placed 6th of 25 teams at Global Game Jam 2026.
